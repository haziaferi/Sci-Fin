# Fresh-eyes audit — 2026-09-25

A read of the whole project that looked for logical fallacies and contradictions, missing features, bloat, and gaps left by earlier passes. It covered all four layers: `scifinspec.md`, `native/`, `tools/`, and the prototype and `design/` as oracles. Every finding was checked by a separate verifier that was told to refute it. Mechanical fixes were applied under the gates. Everything that changes what a person sees or does is listed below for the owner and was **not** changed.

**Findings:** 83 were raised and verified: 75 CONFIRMED, 7 PLAUSIBLE, 1 REFUTED. 10 were the same defect found twice. **26 were fixed in this pass. 46 are open:** 19 behaviour defects, 14 decisions, 4 features, and 9 spec or gate items.

**What to read first.** Four defects are high severity and stop reminders or money movements from happening the way §3.9 and §3.11 promise:

1. A reminder due today at 09:00 is cancelled by any sync that runs earlier that day, such as opening the app at 08:00.
2. The New Month ping cancels itself on the 1st in the same way.
3. The catch-up card never renders after a restart, and it blocks §11.3's goal prompt while it is pending.
4. A recurring expense past its `endDate` loses its last sweeps and its completion notice, because `advanceDueDates` stamps `finishedAt` before the month replay reaches it.

Three of the four were found independently by two different reviewers.

## Method, and what the workflow optimization measured

Five read-only reviewers ran in parallel, and one adversarial verifier then checked each layer's findings. The two **code** reviewers ran different briefs and were *blinded to §13*, the project's own issue register. So there was a measurable question: would they rediscover defects the register already records?

| Recall case (already in §13 or memory) | Brief A: checklist | Brief B: adversarial trace |
|---|---|---|
| K2 `UiState.twoPane` is dead | found | found |
| K3 unscaled `dp` sizes against §2.5 | found | missed |
| K5 no Room corruption handling (§13.1 item 7) | **missed** | **missed** |
| Unique confirmed findings the other brief missed | 12 | 7 |

Neither brief dominated the other: each is the only one to catch some real defects, so the union was kept. Both missed K5, and A missed it even though its checklist says "corruption" outright. The clause asked reviewers to *inspect failure paths*, and an absent hook has no path to inspect. The brief below adds an **absence probe** for that. It merges A and B and is recorded as the brief for the next pass. **It has not been measured yet:** its expected gain on K5 is a prediction, not a result.

## Decisions this pass did not take, and why

**Spec edits.** They were limited to stale status lines where a later dated decision already exists, broken cross-references and wrong figures. Each is struck and dated, following the file's own conventions (`~~…~~`, `corrected 2026\-09\-25`). The dated §13.4 and §14 trail entries were true when written, so they got pointers rather than strikes. Two unstruck rules that conflict with each other, with no later decision between them, are listed as decisions instead:

- the touch-target formula;
- a 44px hit area against the 48dp minimum.

**File rename.** The research audit's file was renamed from its download name `sci-fin-research-audit (1).md` to `sci-fin-research-audit.md`. Four spec citations already used that name, and speccheck now reads the file.
