Budget, as the build actually renders it

Everything I drew before this was inaccurate, in a way that mattered. BudgetScreen.kt:75-79 shows a title row, then a segmented control, then one of Envelopes or Categories — never both. My earlier sheets put both on screen at once and silently dropped the title row, the segmented control, the catch-up card, the learn link, the “received so far” caption, the recurring disclosure note and the whole Goals section. That made the two-column idea look like “split an existing two-pane screen” when it is really “replace the segmented control” — a far bigger claim, and one that has to be argued rather than assumed.

These frames are rebuilt from the composables and filled from prototype-seed.json and its figures block: Savings €3,067, income €1,450 biweekly, six recurring expenses totalling €1,364/mo (car insurance 600÷12 = 50, pest control 90÷2 = 45 — which confirms the €1,364 correction and the earlier €1,095 was wrong), goals Emergency fund 1050/2200, Trip to Lisbon 400/900 and Someday fund with no target, and four categories of which two are over plan. The window is 1084 × 721, the real client area from §14.5.1.

“The app's content is cut” is not a mockup artefact — it is the shipped app at its own default window. The Envelopes view needs 863px and the client area is 721, so 142px — 16% of the screen — is below the fold before anyone touches anything. It is reachable by scrolling, but the Goals section and “New goal” are never visible together with Savings.

And the bar is already at 185:1 today. I had presented 194:1 as what a hypothetical stretched column would do. It is not hypothetical: at the default window the shipped bar is 926dp long and 5dp tall. The distortion the measure cap was meant to prevent is the current state, not a risk.

The two-column proposal improves both — bar to 79:1, near the phone's 62:1, and hidden content from 142px to 94px — but it is not free: it removes the segmented control, which is a §4.3 change, not a §4.8 one. That is the decision, stated properly for the first time in this series.
