# Critique — Sci-Fin Android app, running on the phone

**Artefact.** The debug build (`app.scifin.debug`, APK of 2026-09-15 08:51) on the OnePlus LE2123 — 360 × 804 dp, 480 dpi, phone locale Italian. Reset with `pm clear`, walked from first launch through onboarding, populated entirely through the app's own screens (3 goals, 5 categories, 6 recurring expenses, 2 accounts, a 12-row CSV import, 41 transactions, a second profile), then swept across all four palettes in light and dark. 176 screenshots; contact sheets in `critique-2026-09-15/`.

**Two voices.** Findings marked **measured** come from numbers: WCAG ratios computed from the exact token values in `native/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/scifin/ui/Tokens.kt:42-52` (alpha tokens composited over their ground; table in `critique-2026-09-15/contrast.md`, script `contrast.py`), the device database pulled after the walk, and `craftkit analyse` over every PNG. Everything else is **judgement** from looking at the screens.

---

## Top priorities

1. **[High · measured] Account quick-add shows a Date field and then discards it.** `QuickAddSheet` renders "Date" and the caption "The date affects the ledger and the Reports derived from it" for every target, but for `acct:` targets `quickAddWrite` calls `Accounts.quickAdd` without the date (`ui/sheets/QuickAddSheet.kt:84`, `:107-111`). I dated a Visa charge Sep 1; the ledger row is stored `2026-09-15`. The screen promises something the engine silently ignores — the one class of defect a money app cannot afford.
   *Fix:* either pass `date` through `Accounts.quickAdd` (and let the Owed trend gain history), or hide the Date field and its caption when `account != null` and say so: "Account entries are dated today."

2. **[Med · measured] Two text pairs miss AA — corrected after checking the code.** *Correction (same day):* the first version of this item said `txt3` carried the app's captions; it does not — `Caption` is `txt2` (`Controls.kt:144`), and `txt2` clears 4.5:1 on `bg` and `surf` in all eight palettes, as spec §2.5 says. §2.5 (Decided 2026-09-02) keeps `txt3` decorative-only under WCAG's carve-out, so its 2.16–3.43:1 is a recorded exemption, not a defect. What the measurements actually leave standing: (a) `txt2` on **`surf2`** — the inset ground §2.5 never measured, where `InlineLearn`, `InfoNote` and the disclosure body sit — is 4.44 (Slate), 4.36 (Mauve), 4.11 (Wabi) in light mode; (b) two research-source citations are still drawn in `txt3` (`Progress.kt:160`, `LearnScreen.kt:72`), which §2.5 names as promoted to `txt2`.
   *Fix (applied 2026-09-15):* the three light `txt2` values darkened by the smallest step that clears 4.5 on `surf2` (`#6B6B70`→`#66666B`, `#75636A`→`#6F5D64`, `#6E6A5C`→`#666254`); the two citations moved to `txt2`; `TokenContrastTest` now computes §2.5's table from `Tokens.kt` and forbids a `Text` in `txt3` outside the two placeholder sites.

3. **[High] The app does not own its system bars.** In light mode the status bar is a solid black band above a #F5F5F4 page; in dark mode the navigation bar is pure white below a #1A1A1C page (every `sweep-*-Dark-*` shot). The first and last 7 % of every screen contradict the palette the user chose.
   *Fix:* `enableEdgeToEdge()` in `MainActivity` with `WindowInsets` padding on the frame; set status/navigation bar icon appearance from `tokens.dark`.

4. **[High] Text size xl breaks the layout it is offered for.** At xl (`a11y-Budget-xl`, `a11y-AppearancePage-xl-top`): bottom-nav labels split mid-word ("Report / s", "Setting / s"), the amount "€1,095" wraps to "€1, / 095" in the recurring row, the "→" of "Why split money into categories?" orphans onto its own line, and on the Appearance page the label column of each `SettingRow` collapses to ~63 dp so "System follows your device" stacks one word per line beside the pill.
   *Fix:* `softWrap = false` + `overflow = Ellipsis` on nav labels (or icon-only above lg); amounts as a single unbreakable span (` ` between currency and digits, `maxLines = 1`); give `SettingRow` a minimum label width and let the pill wrap below it when the row is narrower than ~300 dp.

5. **[Med] The permission page is never read.** `PermissionStepScreen` fires the OS `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` dialog in a `LaunchedEffect` the instant it appears, so the page's own rationale ("One more thing before the tour… a single, quiet notification on the 1st of each month") is only ever seen dimmed behind the system dialog (`006-PermissionStep-with-dialog`). The person decides before reading the one sentence that would make them say yes.
   *Fix:* show the page with a "Continue" button; request the permission on tap. This is also Android's recommended pattern (explain, then prompt).

6. **[Med] The currency landing asks one question and buries the answer.** The lead reads "What currency should amounts show in?" — the Currency control is the third card down, after the LOOK grid and Appearance (`016-CurrencyLanding-top`). The page is the real `AppearancePage` with a new title; the title does not match the order.
   *Fix:* on this step only, hoist the Currency row above LOOK, or retitle the lead to what the page actually is ("Make it yours — currency, look, text size. All changeable later.").

7. **[Med · measured] Slate — the default — has no visible accent.** Mean on-screen chroma across the Slate sweep is 0.2 (light) / 0.4 (dark) — the instrument sees a greyscale UI. `acc` #5B6670 sits 16 units of hue from `txt2` #6B6B70, so "Why split money into categories? →", "Why nothing here turns red →", "Show how long this would cover you →" read as bold grey text, not links; the Savings + / ↓ / pencil are neutral discs; the selected tab in the segmented control is white-on-grey. Mauve light is nearly as flat (chroma 0.7). Tide and Wabi carry their accents (4.0–7.3).
   *Fix:* this is a decision, not a defect — a deliberately quiet default is defensible. But then links need a second, non-colour cue (underline on `LearnLink`, or the arrow set in `acc` at heavier weight), and the primary action on each card (Savings "+") should take the `acc` fill Tide already shows.

## Dimension by dimension

### First impression (2-second read)
- **Onboarding sheet** (`001`): the purpose is clear, the register is calm, and the live palette preview — the whole page recolours as you tap a theme card — is the strongest moment of the walk. The disabled "Continue" (muted label on `acc`) is correct but reads as broken on Slate because the enabled state is barely more saturated.
- **Empty Budget** (`018`): a stack of equal grey boxes. The Savings card is the entry point (the note says so) but nothing on it colour-codes "start here"; the eye lands on the INCOME placeholder, the largest grey block, which is the one thing the user cannot act on yet.
- **Populated Budget** (`056`, `084`): good. Savings number, then two links, then the disclosure — the eye path is the right one.

### Visual hierarchy
- **Disclosure note is orphaned** (`057`): "€1,095 recurring · €2,434 Savings" sits between the Savings card and the INCOME header, two sections above the RECURRING EXPENSES row it is about, with no section label of its own. It reads as a stray card. *Fix:* place it directly under the recurring summary row, or give it the `SectionLabel` "RECURRING VS SAVINGS".
- **Goal editor** (`032`): the three sustainability chips (1.5 dp border, `surf2` fill, bold) are the heaviest element on the page, yet they are suggestions; "Suggested: €800/month" — the one number that matters — is a 13.5 sp `txt2` caption below the field.
- **Import review** (`087`): every row is ~300 dp tall because the dropdown's default label "No category — straight from Savings" wraps to two lines; twelve rows put the confirm button a dozen screens down. *Fix:* shorten to "Savings (no category)" — one line — and pin `Cancel` / `Add N entries` to the bottom.
- **Ledger** (`091`): two unlabelled chip groups (six target chips, four range chips, "All" alone on a fourth row) fill the top 40 % of the screen before the first entry; the selected state (light `surf2` fill vs outline) is weak. *Fix:* one row per group, horizontally scrollable, with a 10 sp label; selected chip in `acc` fill + `onAcc` text.
- **Income vs Expenses report** (`099`): the designer's rationale ("One accent, two opacities — income and expenses aren't a good/bad pair") is placed *above* the chart. Explanations belong under the thing they explain.
- **Tutorial stop 3** (`009`): ten lines of copy, twice any other stop. Split into two stops (Recurring / Goals) — the anchor already has two lines.

### Colour and contrast (measured unless noted)
- **AA:** `txt2` on `surf2` missed in Slate/Mauve/Wabi light (4.11–4.44) — fixed the same day, see priority 2. `txt3` sits at 2.16–3.43 on every ground by design (§2.5's decorative-only exemption) and, after the two citation fixes, carries only placeholders. Everything else passes: `txt` 10–17:1, `onAcc`/`acc` 4.6–7.5, `acc` as text on `bg` ≥ 4.5 in all eight, `bordStrong` 3.05–3.08 (tuned to sit just over the non-text line — deliberate and fine).
- **Card against page:** `surf`/`bg` luminance ratio 1.07 (Wabi light) to 1.16 (Tide light). In Wabi light the cards nearly vanish (`sweep-Wabi-Light-Budget`); the spec relies on this delta for separation ("anywhere the surf/bg delta already reads") and in Wabi it does not. *Fix:* Wabi light `surf` → #FBF9F3 (ratio ≈ 1.10) or a `bord` hairline on cards in the light palettes.
- **Palette harmony (judgement):** each palette is internally coherent in both modes; Tide dark is the best-resolved of the eight (accent, ground and text all doing distinct jobs). Dark palettes are uniformly good.
- **Swatch dots don't follow mode** (`sweep-Mauve-Dark-AppearancePage`): the LOOK cards show the *light* accents in dark mode (Slate dot #5B6670, not #8B96A0), so they don't preview what you'd get.
- **Charts use `txt`, not `acc`:** both sparklines (Buffer, Owed) are drawn in text colour — the one place the accent would earn its keep.

### Typography
- Scale contrast between levels is clear: 22/18/16/13.5 sp read as four distinct roles. Body line-height is comfortable (~1.5).
- **Wrapping defects:** "New recurring expense" wraps to two lines in the header (`047`); "WARM, WEATHERED" wraps and makes the Wabi card taller than Tide (`016`); the Density caption "Spacing between rows" is squeezed to three lines by the pill (`016`); "Let's set one up" wraps inside its button while "Not right now" doesn't (`030`); "€1,095" splits at xl (`a11y-Budget-xl`).
- **Text-size control:** the four options are all the same "A" glyph (`016`) — they don't preview their size. Render each at its own scale (A at 14/16/20/24 sp) as every OS does.
- **Section labels** in uppercase tracking are a good, consistent device; the brand glyph beside "Budget"/"Activity"/"Reports" is quiet and right.

### Layout and spacing
- Rhythm is regular: the 8 dp unit shows everywhere, gutters are constant, cards align. Compact density (`a11y-Budget-compact`) tightens without breaking.
- **Bottom nav under sheets** (`030`, `045`): sheets scrim the page but the `NavBar` stays fully bright beneath the scrim, unlike the tutorial where it is dimmed. One rule or the other.
- **Sparkline collides with caption** (`100`): the Owed line starts flush under the last line of caption text with no gap.
- **Icon picker** (`031`, `038`, `047`): 21 pill chips with the same 1.5 dp border weight as the real inputs, for an optional field — it competes with Name and Amount. Lighter treatment (no border, `surf2` fill, selected gets `acc` ring) would keep it optional-looking.

### Copy and microcopy
- The voice is the app's signature — patient, exact, never alarmed — and it is consistent everywhere. The delete sheets ("This month's spend is not un-spent — only the category itself goes away") and the loan sheets ("Real loans pay in arrears, so the first payment is Oct 15") are model microcopy.
- **"(§3.6)"** leaks a spec section number into the quick-add caption (`026`, `071`). Remove.
- **Present tense before the act** (`054`): step 2 of the two-step delete reads "Rent is deleted. Savings stays as it is." while the confirming "Delete" button is still ahead. → "Rent will be deleted. Savings stays as it is."
- **"already clear"** (`068`) captions the "Paid in full this cycle" outcome on a €1,200 balance — it means *would be* clear, reads as *is*. → "cleared at once".
- ~~**"€0 left of goal"** (`037`) → "Funded"~~ — withdrawn: §2.2 mandates that the bar caption "states the difference and nothing else — no verdict, no adjective"; the current caption is the spec's, and the tick already marks the funded state.
- **Stale note** (`100`): "Off by default — with no Credit or Loan accounts, this would just be Savings again" is shown on a screen that is on and has both. Show it only when `accounts.isEmpty()`.
- **Mixed language** (`027`): the M3 date picker follows the phone locale (Italian: "Seleziona data", "Settembre 2026", `DD/MM/YYYY`) while the app and the dialog's own OK/Cancel are English. Either localise the app or pass `Locale.ENGLISH` to the picker until you do.
- **Repeated tail** (`098`): every Spending row says "of this month's outflow". Once in the header is enough.
- **No confirmation after import** (`090`): "Add 12 entries" lands back on the empty import form; the only acknowledgement is the caption "Last import: Sep 15, 2026." → a one-line success state: "12 entries added — €648 out, €240 in. See them in the Activity log →".

### Consistency
- Tokens hold: radii, strokes, the 48 dp targets (every round button dumps at 144 × 144 px = 48 dp) and the section-label style are the same on every screen.
- **Same bar, two meanings** (`041` vs `098`): the category `ProgressBar` means *spent against plan*; in the Spending report the same bar means *share of outflow*. A share deserves a different mark (stacked segments, or a plain figure).
- **Future-dated rows look past** (`091`): "Rent · Sep 28 · Recurring sweep −€900" and "Gym · Sep 30" appear in the log with no "scheduled" marker, indistinguishable from things that have happened.
- **Signature element:** the tick + hatch over-plan encoding (`041`) is present, works, and is explained in place ("Why nothing here turns red →"). It appears only on category bars; the funded-goal tick (`037`) is its sibling. Good — this is the thing to build on.

### Interactivity
- Affordances are clear on the round buttons and dashed "+ New" rows; back is a consistent 48 dp disc.
- **Per-keystroke commit drops input** (`079`–`080`): the per-paycheck amount field writes to the store on every keystroke; typing "2400" at machine speed left "20", slow typing worked. The goal/category editors buffer locally and had no such problem. *Fix:* buffer and commit on blur, like the others.
- **Charts have no scale:** neither sparkline has a baseline, endpoint marker or month ticks (`096`, `100`); the Income chart has no value axis and no headline number — it is the only report without one (`099`). The design forbids alarm colour, not numbers.
- Focus rings and hover could not be checked (touch device).

### Fit for purpose
- The register is right for the spec's "calm register" premise: no red, no exclamation, one accent. The tutorial is short, anchored to live UI, and skippable. The two calculators refuse to create anything without a confirm sheet and say so. This is a design that knows what it is.
- Where it falls short is the *quiet* becoming *mute*: a default palette with no accent, captions below AA, charts without numbers. Calm should still be legible.

## What's working
- Live palette preview on the onboarding sheet — the person sees their choice before committing.
- The over-plan encoding (tick + hatch, explained in place) — the design's one non-colour state mark, and it lands.
- Delete sheets that say exactly what will and will not be reversed, with a two-step path only where money moves.
- The apportioned yearly-expense note ("Sets aside €80 a month until Dec 1, then €20 a month") — a hard idea stated in one sentence at the moment it matters.
- Dark palettes: all four are coherent, Tide dark especially.
- 48 dp targets everywhere, radii and strokes never drift, 8 dp rhythm holds under Compact.

## Cannot verify
- **Wider layouts** (`NavRail` ≥ 600 dp, two-pane ≥ 840 dp) — phone only.
- **`uiScale`** — spec §2.5 (2026-09-15) decided width ÷ 360 dp with no `dp` literals under `ui/`; the code has no global mechanism yet and `OnboardingSheet.kt:66` divides by 332 while its comment says 360. On this 360 dp phone the factor is 1 either way, so nothing here is affected; flagged for the scale pass.
- **`CatchUpCard`**, "Ended" / "paid off" states — need a month boundary; not reached without changing the phone's date.
- **Notifications-denied copy** — needs a "Don't allow" run.
- **Learn topic bodies** — empty by design ("Body copy to be written from the research audit").
- **Focus/hover states**, **reduced motion** (no animations exist to reduce).
- **Walk artefacts, not findings:** the six auto-deposit rows are €20 (the dropped-keystroke field), the Phone expense stayed due on the 15th (my tap hit the picker header), and the Visa APR did not persist from the account editor after I toggled Credit/Loan — resolved: `Accounts.retype` clears the fields of the type it leaves, by spec §7.2.
