Rules that stay

  • Savings only takes money in (owner, 2026-10-09): every expense is spent on a Category, from a Goal, or charged to an Account, so nothing leaves untracked. An imported expense needs a target before import review confirms.
  • Categories only take spending; a correction opens the Category's editor. Goals take money in, or spending up to what they hold.
  • Savings is never set by a sentence; the typed-UPDATE editor stays.
  • A payee that is a recurring expense gets a caption: it is taken on its own date.
  • Confirm and Cancel, never undo; nothing is written before Confirm.
